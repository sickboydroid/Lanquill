# Flow of the app

- Details to enter when they click on either of them
  - Name of user
  - Phone number (for refunds/feedback)
  - Enrollment
  - Password of lanquill
- After entering these details next step will be
  - Shop the items
    - This is where student will select the items to do
    - The selected items total amount is shown
    - After done, if he has selected amount > 0 (i.e some paid item) he is shown the razorpay interface otherwise this step is skipped
      - User might have bought some items already, he should be freely able to add those items without their cost being added

- Then he is shown the progress of his task
  - He is pushed in a queue, he is shown where he is in the queue and about how much time it will take
  - Once his progress starts, he is shown what is being done right now and what n/m (n = number of task m = total tasks asked to do)
  - After done he is shown successfull message and after clicking okay navigated back to the homepage
- User should know that once paid for some tasks, they can do them as many times as they want in future in case the task is not done
